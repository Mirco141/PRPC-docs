# Chain of Command

{% hint style="info" %}
Make sure to follow the Chain of Command (most of the times called "CoC") to avoid punishments!&#x20;

If you need help from your higher ups, go to the next rank above yours in the CoC.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Please take note that:

* After LR, when a MR+ receives more than 2 strikes it will result in a demotion. Even for SHRs.&#x20;
* A Low Rank cannot enter MR while having 2 Strikes.
* If a MR+ gets demoted due to max strikes, the strikes automatically reset.
