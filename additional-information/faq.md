# FAQ

<details>

<summary>What is RDM and Mass RDM?</summary>

**RDM** stands for **Random Death Match**.\
It occurs when a players kills another player randomly, and without a valid roleplay reason.\
\
**Mass RDM**, is the action of executing multiple RDMs. \
In our server, we consider Mass RDM any action that involves killing at least 3 players without a reason, hence committing RDM at least 3 times.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is VDM?</summary>

**VDM** stands for **Vehicle Death Match**.\
It occurs when a players destroys another player's vehicle.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is FRP?</summary>

**FRP** stands for **Fail Role Play**.\
It occurs when a player roleplays unrealistically, and doesn't stay in character.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is FailRP?</summary>

**FailRP** stands for **Fail**ing **R**ole **P**laying.\
It occurs when a player fails to accurately role-play a real life character.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Are costumes allowed in-game?</summary>

**No**, costumes are only allowed for special occasions, specifically only when permission from staff member has been given, or when it is announced.\
Some costumes that still represent real life possible situations, and hence stay in roleplay are allowed, if the roleplay the player is taking part in requires the player to have that costume (example: for halloween).\
However, animals costumes and such are not allowed.\
\
Please ask every player that you meet wearing a costume to remove it, and warn them for "_Not listening to staff orders_" if they don't. You may ask for advice to HR+ staff members if you come across these situations, as they could also decide to give permissions to the player to wear that costume.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Remember:** Sometimes "_it's just a costume_" it's used by players as an excuse to wear unrealistic avatars.
{% endhint %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Am I allowed to use emojis in my messages while on duty?</summary>

No. \
You should only use them in your greetings message to greet someone in a respectful way, and use them the least you can.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Am I allowed to use emotes while on duty?</summary>

No, emotes are completely not allowed while being on duty. \
Although, you may use emojis if you are on break and with other staff members.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is there any moment where I am not required to use SPaG on duty?</summary>

No, due to cases where staff members where caught not using SPaG even in non-formal situations, and to prevent any player from reporting a staff member after seeing them not using SPaG even with other colleagues, **you are always required to use SPaG while on duty**.\
**This doesn't count when you are off duty, of course, and on break**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>If I see a rule breaker off duty, can I do anything to stop them?</summary>

No, you cannot kill for revenge, punish, or execute any command on a rule breaker if you are off-duty, nor you can immediately go on duty an punish them. \
Instead, call a staff member via the "!mod" command, as if you were a normal player, and be patient. You can use staff chat to ask for someone's else help.\
\
The only exception to this rules is when you see an exploiter.\
In this case, you are able to execute a ":kick" (full username required) command or any other action looking to stop the exploiter in the shortest time, on the player if you are <mark style="color:red;">**really**</mark> sure that they are an exploiter.\
If you are unsure on what to do, or more generally in any cases, you should call an High Ranking staff members or above in this type of situations.

{% hint style="danger" %}
If you are caught using this permissions wrongfully you will face harsh punishment such as **Termination** or even **Staff Blacklisted**.\
\
This said, for this kind of situation **you may be required to provide proof that the player you executed an action on was really an exploiter**, hence, it's highly advised to record your screen as proof if you are going to do these actions!
{% endhint %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>What should I do if I Melonly is down and I cannot create logistics?</summary>

When Melonly is down you will be able to use the manual logistics channel that you can find in our Discord server, [here](https://discord.com/channels/735880148125220924/1350305468236431360) <i class="fa-up-right-from-square">:up-right-from-square:</i>.

HR staff members will take care to upload all punishments to the Melonly platform once the service is back.

{% hint style="info" %}
You will be notified by HR staff members whenever Melonly is down. You can also check for yourself at [https://status.melonly.xyz/](https://status.melonly.xyz/)
{% endhint %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>What if a community member has chatting restrictions?</summary>

Private message the person on the game server and tell them to use the new Discord mod call system that they can find in the "[#📞 | mod-calls](https://discord.com/channels/735880148125220924/1452690281425272915)" Discord channel.

If they seem to not be understanding how the system works, or they do not have a Discord account, or they do not want to join our server please let them know that we cannot assist them. It is not our fault.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What if a community member's clip is too long to review?</summary>

You can either ask them to screenshare their screen on a Discord's voice channel, showing the evidence, or reject to see the evidence and operate without it.

{% hint style="info" %}
If the player has evidence and you refuse to watch it, you should not punish anyone and void the scene.
{% endhint %}

Remember that you can always refuse to watch evidence that is suspicious as a file (possible virus) or too long.

</details>
