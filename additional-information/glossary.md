# Glossary

Here are the explanations of some words that you may find in this guide and in your career:

- **RP**<br />The abbreviation for "Roleplay"
- **WL**<br />The abbreviation for "Whitelisted"
- **RDM**<br />The abbreviation for "Random Death Match", see [FAQ](/additional-information/faq#what-is-rdm-and-mass-rdm) for more information.
- **VDM**<br />The abbreviation for "Vehicle Death Match", see [FAQ](/additional-information/faq#what-is-vdm) for more information.
- **Fail RP** or **FRP** or **Fail Roleplay**<br />The abbreviation for "Failing Role Playing", see [FAQ](/additional-information/faq) for more information.
- **NSFW** <br />The abbreviation for "Not Safe For Work", represents all content that is considered prohibited to minors.
