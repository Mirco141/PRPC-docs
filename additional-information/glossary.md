# Glossary

Here are the explanations of some words that you may find in this guide and in your career:

* **RP**\
  The abbreviation for "Roleplay"
* **WL**\
  The abbreviation for "Whitelisted"
* **RDM**\
  The abbreviation for "Random Death Match", see [FAQ](faq.md#what-is-rdm-and-mass-rdm) <i class="fa-up-right-from-square">:up-right-from-square:</i> for more information.
* **VDM**\
  The abbreviation for "Vehicle Death Match", see [FAQ](faq.md#what-is-vdm) <i class="fa-up-right-from-square">:up-right-from-square:</i> for more information.
* **Fail RP** or **FRP** or **Fail Roleplay**\
  The abbreviation for "Failing Role Playing", see[ FAQ](faq.md) <i class="fa-up-right-from-square">:up-right-from-square:</i> for more information.
* **NSFW** \
  The abbreviation for "Not Safe For Work", represents all content that is considered prohibited to minors.

***

Please ask any High Ranking staff member for further explanation if you have any other questions/doubts.
