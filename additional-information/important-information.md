# Important Information

***

## Retirement

When wanting to retire, you can request a retirement via support ticket.

However, the Former Staff role (abbreviated in "FS"), which grants you access to staff chat even without being a staff member, is given only to people who reached a rank equal to TA (Trial Administrator) or above.

Additionally only people who reached an High Ranking (HR) position in their career will have message history in staff chat. This is done to prevent non-staff members leaking staff chat's sensitive content.

***

## Image Permissions

Image Permissions are obtainable by a variety of ways.\
The most easy and common of them is buying them via the economy bot, with the server's currency.\
The other 2 ways includes:

* Becoming an HR staff member, since every HR staff member automatically get Image Permissions;
* Being fortunate enough to receive them as a free gift from SHR+ staff members;

***

## Promotion Cooldowns

For each rank, there is a cooldown which is to be respect between two promotions for each staff member.

They are as it follows:

* Low Ranks (LR) - 3 days cooldown;
* Middle Ranks (MR) - 5 days cooldown;
* Supervisory Department - 1 week cooldown;
* Management Department -  2 weeks cooldown;
* Directive Department - 3 weeks cooldown;
