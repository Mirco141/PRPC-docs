# Staff Awards

#### <mark style="color:$primary;">Commendation Medal:</mark> 

**_Awarded for:_** Outstanding performance that improves PRPC as a whole in certain areas/ways. 

**_Recipient:_** Staff members who constantly perform well and excel in what they do, showing dedication to PRPC.

---

#### <mark style="color:$warning;">Unity Cross Medal:</mark> 

**_Awarded for:_** Showing high levels of teamwork, collaboration and creating positive relationships within the PRPC community.

**_Recipient:_** Staff members who have played a key role in promoting collaboration, fostering positive relationships, and increasing community cohesion.

---

#### <mark style="color:$danger;">Innovation Star:</mark> 

**_Awarded for:_** Finding methods, making features and procedures that help the growth and community of PRPC.

 **_Recipient:_** Staff members who have found new ways/procedures that benefit the community as a whole, and have improved productivity within the community.

---

#### <mark style="color:$warning;">Medal of Honor:</mark> 

**_Awarded for/to:_** Well respected staff of the community and if they had massive contributions towards PRPC. 

**_Recipient:_** Staff members who have consistently demonstrated outstanding dedication, leadership, and service over an extended period. 

---

#### <mark style="color:$success;">Service Cross:</mark> 

**_Awarded for:_** Great Performance on duty and tons of activity.

**_Recipient:_** Staff members who have displayed a high level of activity by going on duty/having high hours.

% hint style="warning" % This is not just staff of the week activity, extreme activity that excels all expectations. % endhint %

---

#### <mark style="color:$success;">Pinnacle Award:</mark> 

**_Awarded for:_** Great department/mafia/PMC management performance

**_Recipient:_** SHR Members who have displayed a high level of activity in the Dept/Mafia/PMC board over a long period of time.

---

#### <mark style="color:$info;">STRN Administration:</mark>

This role is not obtainable. It is only given by the 3 Chairmen if you are trusted. There are no specific requirements to earn this role or work for it. This has to be earned with dedication, unwavering loyalty, service and trust.

---

#### <mark style="color:yellow;">Staff of The Week (SoTW)</mark>

Staff of The Week is awarded each new week to the staff member who has had the highest shift time during the week. It is handed out by te MGT and SHR teams.

The Staff of the Week is granted the following perks for a week (or more, depending on the type of perk):

- Image permissions.
- “ Cool “ role
- 1 Hour off In-game Quota
- Custom emoji
- Custom sticker
- Economy money (10,000,000)

---

#### <mark style="color:green;">Staff of The Month (SoTM)</mark>

Staff of The Month is awarded each new month to the staff member who has had the highest activity during the month, has had the least amount of infractions (In-game, Discord & Staff), has had the most feedback by the Board, and has shown great dedication to the staff team. It is handed out by te SHR and LS councils.

The Staff of the Month is granted the following perks for a month (or more, depending on the type of perk):

- Image permissions 
- “Cool” role 
- 1 hour off in-game quota 
- Custom emoji 
- Custom sticker 
- Banned Vehicles Permissions 
- Banned Weapons Permissions 
- Guaranteed promotion (SMGT and below, if space allows) 
- \$50,000,000 Economy
- 1 Staff or in-game warning/kick removed 
- Up to 3 Discord punishments removed (mute or warning)

% hint style="info" % It is not guaranteed that this is awarded every single month. % endhint %
