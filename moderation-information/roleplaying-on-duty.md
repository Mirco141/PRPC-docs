# Roleplaying On-Duty

Staff members who are Trial Administrator+ may roleplay whilst on duty in minor roleplay scenes. They may also assist in roleplay operations if necessary.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Roleplaying on duty is only to be done when 5+ staff members are on duty.
{% endhint %}

> **For example:**\
> An on duty staff member may check IDs at the border if no Police officers are available, but should not do so when 3+ Police officers are present at the border, instead they must assign someone to handle the border.

On duty staff may not:

* Run for president
* Partake in major roleplays (Such as being taken hostage)

***

### Roleplaying on duty limits

A staff member can only roleplay on duty for up to 25% of their shift.

> **For example**,&#x20;
>
> a Junior Administrator who has been online for 1 hour may roleplay for up to 15 minutes in minor roleplays.

#### Duties first:&#x20;

Staff duties take priority over roleplays, and you are still required to do your staff duties even if you are partaking in minor roleplays. Roleplaying on duty should only be done when there are few mod calls and when you are not hosting a roleplay.

#### On duty ratio:&#x20;

No more than ⅓ of on duty staff members should be roleplaying on duty at any given time, this is to ensure there are still staff available to run normal operations.
