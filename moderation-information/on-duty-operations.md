# On duty Operations

Before proceeding in the guide, you might have asked yourself: "Ok, well, you explained me a lot of things, but what should I do when I join the in-game server?".\
\
If you didn't ask yourself that, here's the answer anyway.

***

## Going on duty

If the server has at least 5 players playing, you are allowed to go on duty as staff member.\
To do so, join the in-game server, join the PRPC Office (Sheriff Team) and put your uniform on via accessing the wardrobe.

Once you're done, you are now ready to go on Melonly's website, and start your duty.<br>

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Join the in-game server


{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Join the "PRPC Office" Team

{% hint style="info" %}
It's the Sheriffs' Team
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take your uniform and car

Head to the wardrobe and take your uniform; make sure to take the one of your role!

If you'd like to take a car (highly advised), head to the parking outside of the station and spawn the car that you like the most (See the list of cars with the staff livery [here](on-duty-protocols.md#your-staff-car) <i class="fa-up-right-from-square">:up-right-from-square:</i>) , with the staff livery you **can** and you want to wear.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Make sure to take only your rank's uniform and livery! You could be punished if you don't do so.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Start your shift

Head to Melonly's website, make sure to be logged in with your Discord account that you also use on PRPC, click on _Dashboard > President Roleplay Community > Moderator Panel_ and click on "Start Shift"&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
You can click on one of the videos below to enlarge them, and see how to start your shift
{% endhint %}

{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take a look at the server's situation

If you are the first one to join after a server shutdown, you can decide which RP to start and what to do. Follow the Roleplays Guidelines for more regulations on which RPs to start in which moments.

If you join between a busy session, or you aren't the only staff member on duty, you should inform yourself about the current server's situation. We advice you to do as follows:

* Check the Discord's category "On Duty", in particular the task channel, and identify who is current host, the highest ranking staff member on duty and any supervisors.
* Check the tasks, and identify any instructions for lately-joiners like you. Sometimes you will be instructed to contact the sender of the tasks (the highest on duty) and other times you will be instructed to just start moderating as normal
* If you still want to be sure about your task, or there is no task, contact the highest on duty (Only IA+) via private message (:pm command) or by teleporting to them and ask them what should you do
* If there is no IA+ on duty, teleport to the other staff members, if you want, and ask them how you could help by organizing each other's job
* If there is a roleplay going on, you should prioritize helping with that roleplay
* If there is no roleplay going on, or if there is a sufficient number of people already helping with the roleplay, start your normal moderation duty
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What to do once on duty

{% hint style="success" %}
Always keep the on duty tasks' Discord channel monitored for new tasks. You will receive a ping when that is updated
{% endhint %}

<details>

<summary>If you are assigned to a specific task</summary>

Follow that task and every instruction that comes with it.

</details>

<details>

<summary>If you are assigned to normal moderation duty</summary>

Your normal moderation duty includes:

* Responding to mod calls;
* Answering players' questions and inquiries;
* Patrolling around to check for wrongly parked cars or any suspicious situation;
* Supervising roleplays and checking that everyone is respecting the rules;

You should prioritize these tasks rather than helping with the RP hosting unless you are asked to do so, or there is a chaos situation going on.

</details>

<details>

<summary>If you are assigned to hosting/co-hosting the roleplay</summary>

In this case, you should prioritize supervising and managing anything related to the ongoing roleplay.

This includes:

* Making frequent announcements on the current RP situation;
* Checking if everything in the RP is going on correctly and if everyone is following the rules;
* Acting in case of rule-breaking players that make the RP unfair;
* Coordinating the RP procedures, making decisions for the RP, coordinating other staff members that are helping with the RP
* Making sure that the RP can proceed without interruption;
* Responding to questions specifically related to the RP;
* Completing RP-case tasks, listed in the [_Roleplays Guidelines_](/broken/pages/y7JnFfA3MR7DWVZX6qa2) <i class="fa-up-right-from-square">:up-right-from-square:</i> section. These change for each RP;

As you can see, if you're the host/co-host you will have far more tasks from the normal moderation duty, but don't worry! It isn't all on yourself!&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
These tasks should be divided autonomously by the host and co-host together
{% endhint %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>If you don't have any task</summary>

If there is no current RP ongoing and if there is a sufficient number of players to host an RP (at least 5-10 players) you have 2 possibilities:

* If the number of players is lower than 20, host a Border or Normal RP at your choice;
* If the number of players is 20 or higher:
  * Especially if there is chaos in the server, host an RP Vote to let people decide which roleplay they prefer playing;
  * Otherwise, you are highly advised to host President RP;

{% hint style="info" %}
Find instructions on how to host these RPs in the _Roleplays Guidelines_ section
{% endhint %}

</details>
