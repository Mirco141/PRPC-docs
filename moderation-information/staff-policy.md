# Staff Policy

***

At the foundation of our staff team is **professionalism**. If you want to be promoted, remember that the key isn't shift time or activity only, we value your professionalism and capabilities of handling situations during your duties.

We expect a lot from you, staff members, because you are the face of PRPC, the ones that determines if a player stays with us or not because they're the ones that will speak and ask you questions.

## **Fundamental Rules**

* Always act professional.
* Don’t share personal opinions that could sound biased.
* Dress nicely, and make the staff team look good.
* Use proper words, not slang or short forms, in official messages or on duty.
* Use good spelling, punctuation, and grammar while on duty.
* Stay calm, don't get angry when moderating. If it's too much, take a break.
* Don’t do silly things like dancing or using casual words like “ bro “ while on duty.
* Behave in game and in Discord, even off duty. Just because your staff, it doesn't mean you get free rule bypasses!

{% hint style="info" %}
These are heavily recommended to follow not only on duty, but even in the discord. When creating staff shouts, punishing, promoting or putting something on the record you are required to follow theses, even on duty. Staff are meant to be formal, not acting silly.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
Just because you're staff, it doesn't mean you can decide whenever you follow the rules or not. Staff in-game punishments and Discord are relatively stricter than community members. As staff uphold PRPC and should be the leading example. If you aren’t happy with this, please reconsider before committing to staff. Staff isn't always fun, and you should be ready to see things you’re uncomfortable with, especially when getting access to logs and becoming MR+.
{% endhint %}

## **Must-Know for your career**

* Know all the server rules by heart.
* Use good judgement when handling problems.
* Treat everyone the same and follow the rules fairly, even if they aren’t doing it to you.
* If you’re in trouble, write down what happened and explain what you did calmly and clearly.
* Always tell HR+ If something serious happens. Don’t hesitate.

{% hint style="info" %}
Since we can't write everything about every single situation in this guide, you will learn how to use your personal judgment.\
Judgement is the most important thing, and don’t expect to instantly know how to judge perfectly! It will take time. \
\
If you’re unsure about something, ping or DM your superiors and ask them **nicely**. Use your judgement to decide what's best for a situation and look at all sides.
{% endhint %}

***

## Communication Fundamentals

* Speak clearly and politely, using good spelling and grammar;
* Be friendly and respectful to everyone;
* Don't be rude, swear or say hurtful things;
* Be nice at all times;
* Always treat everyone with respect;

***

## Restrictions & Staff Memberships

* Only handle tasks within your role, don't go beyond your permissions
* Use common sense, don't touch sensitive controls
* &#x20;Represent PRPC Staff on other servers, respect their rules
* Staff must include their Roblox name in their Discord username (MGT+ can use related names instead)
* Staff can't use custom nicknames as membership perk
* Staff role colors must be unique, not white or black, and not match existing roles colours
* Custom roles must not sound as official ranks
* Only IA+ may add time zones to nicknames
* Follow the correct nickname in the Discord, Rank・User&#x20;

***

## Discipline

* Always act polite and set a good example;
* Follow all staff rules at all times;
* Do not swear, even if you’re allowed to;
* Follow the Chain of Command at all times, ask higher ups when help is required but go to the appropriate rank (don't directly go to Vice-Chairmen if an HR can help you);

***

## Uniforms

* Wear the uniform of your rank (SHR+ Can bypass this)
* Only wear the official uniform, no edited or custom ones;
* Wear the uniform ONLY on duty, not off-duty;
* Keep your avatar realistic, no silly avatars or unprofessional ones;
* Always use SPaG when wearing a staff uniform.

***

## Promotions

* Don't ask or hint for promotions;
* Being active helps, but good behavior and professionalism matter more, as well as following these principles;
* Never target someone because they got a rank you wanted, be patient and wait your turn. (It usually only ends in you getting punished!)
