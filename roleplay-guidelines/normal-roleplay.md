# Normal Roleplay

Normal Roleplay is the normal activity of role-playing real life characters.

We prefer not to host this RP as we promise our players that we are a President Roleplay server, but you can host this type of roleplay in one of the following cases:

* There isn't a sufficient amount of staff members on-duty (less than 3)
* There isn't a sufficient amount of players online, staff members excluded (less than 15 _usually_)
* There is a condition for which you are not able to host President RP

{% hint style="info" %}
Remember you can also choose to host Border RP instead of Normal RP when President RP can't be hosted.
{% endhint %}

***

During a Normal RP, your only job is making sure that every players is respecting the rules correctly, and completing your normal duty tasks. See [On-Duty Operations](../moderation-information/on-duty-operations.md#what-to-do-once-on-duty) <i class="fa-up-right-from-square">:up-right-from-square:</i> for more information.

Some events may be hosted by authorized Event Hosts during this roleplay. Additionally, Whitelisted RPs might also be hosted during this roleplay.

In both cases, follow the instruction of the Event Host or the requests of the Whitelisted RP's coordinator.
