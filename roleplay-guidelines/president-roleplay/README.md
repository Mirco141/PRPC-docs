# President Roleplay

Presidential Roleplay (Often abbreviated as President RP) is the main RP that our server hosts, and is the main reason that people come to our server.

This is why, hosting President RP is a serious job and requires professionalism and attention to details. We are always in search of high-quality RPs, especially for President RP and therefore we look to have specialized staff members for this task.

***

The following guide is divided in smaller pages due to its high content quantity.\
This is done to improve readability, and to less confuse your mind while reading.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Remember!** This guide goes into even the smallest deatil to avoid misunderstanding of procedure and to regulate how everything should be done, but the most important thing is that you know how to do what you are asked to do in-game.

You can always come back later to check something, even while you're already hosting the roleplay!
{% endhint %}
