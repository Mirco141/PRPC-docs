# Staff Role Subdvision

To ensure an organized hosting of the President RP, our server adopts a role subdivision for our on-duty staff members at the moment of the RP happening.

{% hint style="warning" %}
To host a President RP, at least 2 (advised 3) staff members must be on-duty, and 10 (advised 15) players must be online. You can stop a President RP at any time for lack of staff on-duty or lack of players online.
{% endhint %}

## Role Assignment procedure

The role subdivision among on-duty staff members can be assigned in either of the two following ways:

* Decided and assigned by an High Ranking staff members, or a Middle Ranking staff member delegated by an High Ranking staff members through verbal or written permissions;
* Automatically assigned following a general rule, which follows below:
  * The highest on duty rank below the HR ranks, or any volunteer, will become the **host**. \
    <mark style="color:$danger;">Trial Moderators (TMs) cannot be host</mark>. Anyone above JM+ may volunteer to be one.
  * Any volunteer, chosen between on duty staff members, <mark style="color:$primary;">which is ranked above TM+</mark> should become the **co-host**. \
    <mark style="color:$primary;">LR staff members are preferred to be one</mark>, as MR and HR might have other tasks to do, like supervising.
  * Everyone else remaining without a role, on-duty, should assist the host and co-host with the RP hosting, and also respond to mod calls and players inquiries. \
    Staff members may also decide to divide this generalized role in two different roles, <mark style="color:$danger;">if the number of on-duty staff members is higher than 3</mark> <mark style="color:$danger;background-color:red;">(> 3 NOT ≥ 3)</mark>, as follows:
    * A group of staff members being the **supporters**
    * The remaining ones being the **moderation** subdivision

{% hint style="info" %}
That might have been a little bit hard to read and understand, but don't worry, you will get used to this role subdivision system!&#x20;
{% endhint %}

Now, we're left with 4 type of roles, which will be explained below.

***

## Tasks for each role

### The Host and Co-Host

The host and co-host should prioritize supervising and managing anything related to the roleplay, and completely ignore (when possible) other ongoing situations in the server.

This includes, for example, but are not limited to:

* Hosting the Elections;
* Manage the Staff <--> Players communications about the President's lives, locations and more (generally communicating to the players what to do, where, and any changes in the RP situation);
* Making frequent announcements on the current RP situation;
* Checking if everything in the RP is going on correctly and if everyone is following the rules;
* Coordinating the RP procedures, making decisions for the RP, coordinating other staff members that are helping with the RP
* Making sure that the RP can proceed without interruption;
* Leading the convoy, choosing its route, and organizing other staff members in order to block its route from extra-RP vehicles.
* Confirm or invalid the kills of the President;
* Handle the replacement of the President in case they go offline, or they finish their lives;

As you can see, if you're the host/co-host you will have far more tasks from the normal moderation duty, but don't worry! It isn't all on yourself!&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
These tasks should be divided autonomously by the host and co-host together
{% endhint %}

Lastly, the host and co-host may assign some of their task to be divided or completely done by other staff members, as long as these are secondary task, there is a sufficient and reasonable need, and that the other staff member isn't busy with other tasks.

If the host goes on break, or off-duty, the co-host automatically becomes the host if possible (and if unless specified by high ranking staff members), either temporary (for breaks) or permanently (for end of shifts). A new co-host must be choosen between staff members, by an high ranking staff members if there is one online, or by the whole on-duty staff members in case there isn't.

{% hint style="success" %}
A host/co-host is only punished when zero efforts to maintain a well-going RP is shown. In other cases, we know it's an hard task and difficult for many of you. \
\
For the same reason, we **highly advise** the host and co-host to ask for some help to other online staff members (especially experienced ones).
{% endhint %}

***

### The Supporters

This refers to the group of staff members assigned (by an HR or automatically) to side-tasks of the main President RP.

These staff members may focus on activities regarding the main President RP. <mark style="background-color:$primary;">They may avoid completing any normal duty task,</mark> <mark style="color:red;background-color:$primary;">only if there is someone assigned to the Moderation Subdivision</mark>.

They are meant to help the host and co-host to host the RP, in any task they are asked to complete, or they seem reasonable to complete.

These tasks includes, for example, but are not limited to:

* Any task that is asked to be completed by the host or co-host;
* Answering players' doubts regarding the ongoing roleplay;
* Monitoring the President for any kill, and communicating that kill to the host and co-host for further handling;
* Helping to decide whether or not a kill of the President was valid;
* Leading and setting up the convoy, if asked to do so;
* Acting in case of rule-breaking players that make the RP unfair;

***

### The Moderation Subdivision

The Moderation Subdivision is NOT equal to the Moderation team (the staff team composed of Moderation ranks).

This refers to the group of staff members that are assigned (by an HR or automatically) to the task of normally moderating the server, even during a President Roleplay.

These staff members should continue their normal job that includes:

* Responding to mod calls;
* Answering players' doubts regarding any matter;
* Patrolling around to check that every car is parked correctly;
* Patrolling around to check that everyone is playing (including people playing in side roleplays) according to the rules;
* Follow any order or sub-task given by HR+ staff members;
* If asked, supervising a Whitelisted RP;
* Making sure that people without permissions aren't using permission-only guns and vehicles;
* Making sure every player is wearing a realistic avatar;
* Making sure that everyone is respecting the server's rules in any way and form;

{% hint style="info" %}
These staff member may also help with the main President RP if asked by one of the staff member assigned to that role, and if available.&#x20;

These may also help with minor tasks about handling the President RP, but <mark style="color:red;">must prioritize normal duty operation</mark><mark style="color:$info;">.</mark>
{% endhint %}
