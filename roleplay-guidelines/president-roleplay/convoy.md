# Convoy

The Convoy is the formation of cars that moves with the President, whenever he decides to move from place A to place B.

A convoy in our server must follow strict rules, and must have an organized route before departure.

In this section of the guide we will list all the rules and procedures that you must follow when handling a convoy in-game.

***

## Convoy rules and constraints

A convoy must:

* Not have more than 8 non-staff vehicles (usually)
* Have a maximum of 2 limousines, one which carriers both the President and Vice-President, the other being the decoy
* Have a maximum of 2 staff vehicles, one at the very front, and the other at the very back
* Not have any civilians in the convoy
* Be composed of only blacked out vehicles, meaning that all vehicles must have a black livery without any writing on them (an exception is made for Whitelisted Departments vehicles)

During its travel a convoy and its members must follow the following rules:

* No overtaking unless the vehicle in front is stuck, AFK, or if a staff members gives permission to do so
* No sirens, honking, or ramming into other vehicles
* All convoy roads must be fully blocked to prevent any external disturbance (unless if strictly not possible)
* No civilian should drive in or around the convoy
* Maintain a speed between 15-30 mph, or 15-30 Km/h

{% hint style="warning" %}
Please remember that we have strict punishments about people disturbing the convoy, refer to the [Punishments Guide](../../moderation-information/punishments-guide.md) <i class="fa-up-right-from-square">:up-right-from-square:</i> for further information.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
Instantly jail anyone who:

* Overtake during the convoy
* Drive near or in the convoy
* Is trying to drive into the convoy (prevent this from happening)
* Disturbs the convoy in general
{% endhint %}

Sometimes you are allowed to permit more than 8 vehicles in a convoy.

***

## Convoy procedures&#x20;

During a convoy you should **always** make sure that a Peace Timer is active to prevent anyone from shooting at the convoy.

{% code overflow="wrap" %}
```
:pt 500
```
{% endcode %}

The President cannot be killed during a convoy.

If the President's vehicle decides to drive off and away without staff consent, the President can then be killed, but you must stop the convoy **immediately** and teleport to his vehicle to get him back on track.
