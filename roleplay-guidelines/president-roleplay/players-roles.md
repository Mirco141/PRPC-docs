# Players Roles

## Players role subdivision

Players in President RP divides in the current roles:

* The President
* The Vice-President
* The Guards
* The LEOs
* The Civilians

They are all pretty self-explanatory, but we will spend some time on specifying their role in the RP.

***

### The President

The President is the main character of this Roleplay type. This role is the representation of the real life role of "President of the United States of America".&#x20;

The President is elected at first, through elections, by the players of PRPC.\
Later on, when the President finishes all of his lives or goes offline, the Vice-President, if still online and available, will take the role.

{% hint style="info" %}
A President has 3 lives, meaning he can be killed only 3 times in 1 RP.
{% endhint %}

The RP revolves around the President. Players have the task to protect the President, while some others may decide to try to kill him. The President also decides wherever he wants to go, and what to do.

{% hint style="info" %}
The President has no real political power over the server, meaning that he can't take decisions, modify rules, allow players to do something usually not allowed, etc...
{% endhint %}

***

### The Vice-President

The Vice-President is the second most elected candidate at the elections at first. He later serves as the replacement for an unavailable President.

This role is the representation of the real life role of "Vice-President of the Unites Stated of America".

In this RP, the Vice-President have no power on anything, nor can decide what to do and where to go.\
He can only decide to remain and follow the President in his moves, or play as a normal player until it's his turn to become President.

***

### The Guards

The Guards are a small group of players who spountanously decide to become the personal Guards of the President.&#x20;

These are not regulated by the staff of PRPC, as their job is spuntanous and they can decide what to do at any time. We do not require players to become Guards.

{% hint style="info" %}
A President can also have no Guards at all.
{% endhint %}

You might see a group of people specifically protecting the President staying near him and with him at all times, and sometimes dressed diferrently from the LEOs. Those are Guards.

{% hint style="success" %}
Sometimes someone may ask you "Can I be the President's guard?".

You should answer them that it's their choice to become one, as long as the President himself is fine with that.
{% endhint %}

***

### The LEOs

LEO stands for Low Enforcement Officer. This is the group that represents Police Officers and Sheriffs Officers (people who enforce the law), and sometimes (like in this case) also include EMS and Fire Department people.

#### <i class="fa-chevron-right">:chevron-right:</i>Police Officers and Sheriffs Officers

Police Officers and Sheriffs Officers are able to participate to the RP as they want and if they want.

Typically, they set up checks around the area where the President is, form security areas and protect the area from general attacks.

{% hint style="info" %}
They are different from the Guards because they don't specifically protect the President only, but they protect the entire area and city.
{% endhint %}

#### <i class="fa-chevron-right">:chevron-right:</i>EMS and Fire Department

Like Police Officers and Sheriffs Officers they are able to participate to the RP as they want and if they want.

They serve their normal role of extingushing fires and healing people.

{% hint style="danger" %}
EMS are allowed to heal anyone on the RP scene apart from the President.

The President, once killed, can only be healed by the staff members via command.
{% endhint %}

#### <i class="fa-chevron-right">:chevron-right:</i>DOT

DOT members can participate to the RP as they want and if they want, and serve their normal role of fixing city-controlled infrastracture, repairing cars and refueling vehicles.&#x20;

They are also allowed, and are really welcome, to set up the convoy's path barriers with their available items. They are also useful whenever the President's limousine is out of fuel.

#### <i class="fa-chevron-right">:chevron-right:</i>Whitelisted Departments

Whitelisted Departments sometimes want to participate in the RP too. As you may see in our list of whitelisted departments, we have some private protection companies.

These last ones, specifically, tend to sometimes participate to the RP to further protect the President and the area around. <mark style="color:green;">They are completely allowed to do so.</mark>

Sometimes you might also see mafias that in group, try to kill the President. <mark style="color:green;">They are allowed to do so</mark> unless differently specified by an HR+ staff member.

***

### The Civilians

The civilians are the remaining people who choose not to play as LEO or don't become President/Vice-President.

These typically divides in 2 smaller groups:

* The Spectators - People who normally just watch the RP going on, try to talk to the President, etc.
* The Killers - People who always try to find a way to kill the President

Both roles of civilians are allowed, as long as they are not breaking rules and disturbing the roleplay procedure.

***

{% hint style="warning" %}
Everyone can switch between roles as many times as they want and whenever they want during the RP, apart from the elected roles which are the President and Vice-President.
{% endhint %}
